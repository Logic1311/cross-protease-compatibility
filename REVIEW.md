## Acceptance decision

Mathematical identity and its finite-data application pass the computational review. The result supports a modest but substantive reproducible methods preprint. It does not establish a new thermodynamic law, calibrated simultaneous interval coverage, or generalization to independently sampled protein families. No journal review or human authorship is claimed. Site pairs were selected in the source, and the two protease conditions share modeling and measurement systematics. Extra tolerances do not estimate actual assay error. This version replaces the broader claims of the preliminary work.
